How to Play Yahtzee

Roll 5 dice up to 3 times per turn. After each roll, you may hold any dice you want to keep. After rolling at least once, select a scoring category.

Upper Section

  • Ones – Sixes: Sum of dice showing that number
  • Bonus: Score 35 extra if upper total ≥ 63

Lower Section

  • Three of a Kind: 3+ same → sum all dice
  • Four of a Kind: 4+ same → sum all dice
  • Full House: 3 of one + 2 of another → 25
  • Sm. Straight: 4 sequential dice → 30
  • Lg. Straight: 5 sequential dice → 40
  • Yahtzee: All 5 the same → 50
  • Chance: Sum of all dice

Yahtzee Bonus

If you roll another Yahtzee after scoring 50 in Yahtzee, you earn +100 bonus each time (up to 3). The Joker Rule also lets you use it in any lower section category at full value if the matching upper category is filled.
