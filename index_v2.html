How to Play

Objective

Score as many points as possible across 13 turns by filling in every category on the scorecard. Highest grand total wins.

Taking a Turn

  • Click Roll Dice to roll all 5 dice. You get up to 3 rolls per turn.
  • After each roll, click any die to hold it (gold border = held). Held dice are kept on your next roll.
  • After 1–3 rolls, click a category on the scorecard to record your score and end the turn.
  • If no category fits, scratch any open category to score 0 and move on.

Upper Section

  • Ones – Sixes: Sum of all dice showing that face value.
  • Bonus +35: Earned if your upper section total reaches 63 or more (average of 3 per face).

Lower Section

  • Three of a Kind: At least 3 matching dice — score = sum of all 5 dice.
  • Four of a Kind: At least 4 matching dice — score = sum of all 5 dice.
  • Full House: 3 of one value + 2 of another — 25 pts.
  • Small Straight: 4 sequential dice (e.g. 2-3-4-5) — 30 pts.
  • Large Straight: 5 sequential dice (1-2-3-4-5 or 2-3-4-5-6) — 40 pts.
  • Yahtzee: All 5 dice the same — 50 pts.
  • Chance: Any roll — score = sum of all 5 dice. Use as a fallback.

Yahtzee Bonus

Roll a second (or third…) Yahtzee after already scoring 50 in the Yahtzee box? Each extra Yahtzee earns +100 bonus points and acts as a joker — it counts as a valid Full House, Small Straight, or Large Straight too.
