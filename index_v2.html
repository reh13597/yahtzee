How to Play Yahtzee

Each game consists of 13 turns. On each turn, you roll 5 dice up to 3 times. After your first roll, you may click any dice to hold them — held dice won't be re-rolled. After rolling at least once, you must choose one unused scoring category to record your score.

Upper Section

  • Ones: Add up all dice showing 1. (e.g., 1-1-3-4-6 = 2 pts)
  • Twos: Add up all dice showing 2. (e.g., 2-2-2-5-6 = 6 pts)
  • Threes: Add up all dice showing 3.
  • Fours: Add up all dice showing 4.
  • Fives: Add up all dice showing 5.
  • Sixes: Add up all dice showing 6.
  • Upper Bonus: If your upper section total is 63 or more, you earn a +35 point bonus. Tip: aim for 3 of each number to hit 63.

Lower Section

  • Three of a Kind: At least 3 dice showing the same number. Score = sum of all 5 dice. (e.g., 4-4-4-2-6 = 20 pts)
  • Four of a Kind: At least 4 dice showing the same number. Score = sum of all 5 dice.
  • Full House: Three of one number and two of another (e.g., 3-3-3-5-5). Scores a flat 25 points.
  • Small Straight: Four dice in a row (e.g., 1-2-3-4, 2-3-4-5, or 3-4-5-6). Scores a flat 30 points.
  • Large Straight: All five dice in a row (1-2-3-4-5 or 2-3-4-5-6). Scores a flat 40 points.
  • Yahtzee: All 5 dice showing the same number. Scores 50 points!
  • Chance: No requirements — just add up all 5 dice. Useful as a fallback when nothing else fits.

Yahtzee Bonus & Joker Rule

If you've already scored 50 in the Yahtzee category and roll another Yahtzee, you earn a +100 point bonus (up to 3 times). You must still choose a category for the turn — if the matching upper category is open, you must use it. Otherwise, you may use any open lower section category at its full point value (this is the "Joker Rule").

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Space / Enter: Roll the dice
  • 1–5: Toggle hold on each die
  • Esc: Close this window
