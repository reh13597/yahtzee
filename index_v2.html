How to Play Yahtzee

Each game consists of 13 rounds. Every round, you roll 5 dice up to 3 times. After any roll, you may hold dice you want to keep by clicking them, then roll again. After at least one roll, choose a scoring category on the scorecard to lock in your points for that round.

Upper Section

  • Ones through Sixes: Add up only the dice showing that number. For example, if you have three 4s, your "Fours" score would be 12 (3 × 4). Tip: aim for at least three of each number to reach the bonus threshold.
  • Upper Bonus (+35): If your upper section total is 63 or more, you earn a 35-point bonus. Scoring three of each number (1+1+1 + 2+2+2 + ... + 6+6+6) gives exactly 63.

Lower Section

  • Three of a Kind: At least 3 dice showing the same value. Score = sum of all five dice. Try to get three of a high number for maximum points.
  • Four of a Kind: At least 4 dice showing the same value. Score = sum of all five dice.
  • Full House: Three of one number and two of another (e.g., 3-3-3-5-5). Always scores 25 points.
  • Small Straight: Four dice in a row (e.g., 1-2-3-4, 2-3-4-5, or 3-4-5-6). Always scores 30 points.
  • Large Straight: All five dice in a row (1-2-3-4-5 or 2-3-4-5-6). Always scores 40 points.
  • Yahtzee: All five dice showing the same value. Scores 50 points — the highest single category!
  • Chance: No requirements — simply add up all five dice. Use this as a fallback when nothing else fits well.

Yahtzee Bonus

Already scored 50 in Yahtzee and rolled another one? You earn a +100 bonus each additional Yahtzee (up to 3 times). Plus, the Joker Rule lets you place it in any open lower section category at full value, as long as the matching upper section number is already filled.
